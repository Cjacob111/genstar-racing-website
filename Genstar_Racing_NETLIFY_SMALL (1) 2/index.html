THE PROCESS

How It Works

01. Choose your horse

Review photos, pedigrees and available shares in our horses.

02. Select your ownership

Choose a share percentage and submit your request. Complete the required ownership agreement before purchase.

03. Understand your costs

The base fee is $20 USD per 1% per month. Budget an estimated additional $5 USD per 1% for variable incidentals; actual expenses may be higher or lower.

04. Experience the stable

Receive horse updates, schedule barn visits, attend race days and track your applicable net purse earnings in your owner account.

All prices, fees, expenses and earnings are in U.S. dollars (USD) unless otherwise noted. Racing carries financial risk; earnings are not guaranteed.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Ownership FAQ

What does monthly care cover?

Beginning November 1, 2026, the $20 per 1% monthly base charge covers training, boarding, daily care and supplies. We estimate another $5 per 1% monthly for incidentals, for a total estimate of $25 per 1%.

Are there additional expenses?

Yes. The additional $5 per 1% is an estimate, not a cap. Actual expenses can differ and stakes or extraordinary charges may be billed separately under the final agreement.

Can share prices change?

The initial prices shown are current offering valuations and may be updated before a sale. Members set their own asking prices for resale shares.

Is this open to the public?

The current concept is invitation-only for friends and family. Members may list shares at their chosen price, subject to eligibility, payment and transfer requirements.

What your initial share price includes

Each horse’s initial offering valuation includes the original purchase price, a 10% acquisition markup, and an additional $1,000 per horse covering vetting, breaking, shipping and carrying costs through October 31, 2026. No separate carrying-cost bills apply before November 1, 2026.

Current offering valuations: Rocco's Return $10,000 · Dr. Philippon $15,500 · Redeemed $12,000 · Peace Be WithYou $31,000.
